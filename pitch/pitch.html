Rebuttals

Isn't this just Pay.sh?
Pay.sh is a catalog — what exists, at what price. A market adds routing, reputation, refund-on-failure, budget policy, and human supply. We federate their 72 providers rather than compete with them.
Isn't this just x402?
x402 is the handshake, not the marketplace. It's the rail. Rails commoditize; the trust layer on top keeps its margin.
Why not give the agent a credit card?
Cards can't process a third of a cent — the fee exceeds the charge. And no bank issues an account to software.
Why does this need a chain at all?
Software has to hold money and pay other software, in fractions of a cent, instantly, with no account relationship. Nothing else does all four.
What stops sellers returning garbage?
Reputation from transaction history and escrow with refund-on-failure — 30-day roadmap. Tonight the budget cap is the guard.

Rebuttals — cont.

Prompt injection from seller responses?
Seller output is treated as untrusted data, never as instructions. Schema-validated, sandboxed.
Cold start — where's the supply?
72 providers on night one, federated. The supply problem is the one we didn't have to solve.
The human step is slow.
It's the path for what no API can do. The alternative isn't faster — it's the task failing.
Stripe, Coinbase, Visa are all shipping this.
They're shipping rails. Rails commoditize. The market and the reputation layer is a different business — and it's only earnable over transaction history.
Is the head count real?
Real vision model if the key's live, otherwise a stated mock. Know which mode you're in before you walk up — don't guess on stage.
